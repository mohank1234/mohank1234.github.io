Personal Job Automation

Tools that help the owner manage a personal job search, maintain a Naukri profile, and receive updates about completed work.

This page explains the application associated with the Google consent screen. It is an information page, with no public sign-up or account-connection form.

What the application does

  • Runs scheduled Naukri profile maintenance and sends the owner a confirmation after a saved change has been verified.
  • Reads fresh Naukri verification emails when an authorized login requires a one-time code.
  • Supports personal job-search reporting and outreach workflows, including preparing Gmail drafts and checking for replies before configured follow-ups.
  • Uses selected Google Drive reports and supporting documents, and creates or updates app-owned reports when the owner enables the separate reporting connection.

Why Google access is requested

Gmail access is used for verification messages, workflow notifications, drafts, and reply checks. Optional Drive access supports the owner's job-search reports and documents. The Naukri notification connection requests Gmail permissions; separate job-search features may also request Drive permissions.

Google shows the permissions during sign-in. Automation runs only with the account owner's authorization. See the privacy policy for how data is accessed, used, stored, and shared.

Account control and support

The owner can stop scheduled workflows or revoke the application's Google access through Google Account connections.
